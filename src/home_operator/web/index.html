What can I ask?

This is a demo home with five real appliances. Everything Home Operator knows about them was read from each manufacturer's manual by Amazon Bedrock, then checked by a person against the page. It can look things up, diagnose problems, and walk you through a repair one step at a time.

A two-minute demo
  1. “My washer won't drain.”It diagnoses from the LG manual and offers to walk you through cleaning the drain pump filter.
  2. “Yes, walk me through it.”Step 1 is a safety gate: turn off and unplug the washer. The ring turns amber.
  3. “Next.”It refuses. The gate only clears when you say the machine is off.
  4. “Tell me step four.”Still refuses: you can't skip past a safety step.
  5. “Okay, it's unplugged.”Now it moves to step 2, word for word from page 40 of the manual.
  6. “Skip to step five.” · “Go back.” · “Say that again.”Move around freely once it's safe. Interrupt it any time.
  7. “What filter does the furnace take?”Ask something else mid-repair. Your place is kept.
